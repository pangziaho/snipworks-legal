Support

SnipWorks · Updated 5 October 2026

Questions or problems? Email softnjer@yeah.net. Please include your Mac model, macOS version and what you were capturing.

Common questions

Nothing happens when I press the shortcut, or I only see the wallpaper.

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on SnipWorks, then quit and reopen SnipWorks (macOS only applies the permission after a restart of the app).

macOS keeps asking whether SnipWorks may keep accessing the screen.

Since macOS 15, the system asks about once a month for every screenshot and screen-recording app. It’s a system privacy check that apps can’t turn off — choose to keep allowing.

How do I use ⌘⇧3 / ⌘⇧4 / ⌘⇧5?

In Settings › Shortcuts, choose Use ⌘⇧3 / 4 / 5. Then turn off the screenshot shortcuts in System Settings › Keyboard › Keyboard Shortcuts › Screenshots; otherwise macOS responds first. SnipWorks shows a warning next to any shortcut macOS is still using.

My scrolling capture says “too fast” or has a gap.

Scroll a little more slowly, and scroll back a bit when asked. Select only the part that scrolls. Sticky headers and footers are detected automatically. For chat histories, start at the bottom and scroll up.

Where are my captures?

Saved captures go to Pictures/SnipWorks and recordings to Movies/SnipWorks (change it in Settings › Save). Every capture is also in Capture History, where you can search the text inside images.

Which languages can text recognition read?

Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Japanese, Korean, English and other languages supported by macOS, including mixed and vertical text.

A pin doesn’t respond to clicks.

It is locked so clicks pass through. Unlock it from the SnipWorks menu in the menu bar.

Do I need to buy it again on my other Macs?

No. It’s a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store and works on every Mac signed in with the same Apple Account.
