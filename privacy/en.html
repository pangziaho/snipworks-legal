Privacy Policy

SnipWorks · Updated 5 October 2026

SnipWorks captures, marks up, recognizes and stores everything on your Mac. The app has no network access at all: your screenshots, recordings and recognized text never leave your Mac unless you save, copy or share them yourself. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What the app accesses

  • Screen & System Audio Recording. macOS requires this permission for any screenshot tool. SnipWorks reads the screen only when you start a capture, a scrolling capture or a recording, and only keeps what you capture.
  • System audio (optional). Recorded only when you turn on Record System Audio for a screen recording.
  • Microphone (optional). Used only when you turn on Record Microphone for a screen recording.
  • Files. Captures are saved to Pictures/SnipWorks and recordings to Movies/SnipWorks, or to a folder you choose.
  • Capture history (optional). Captures, markup and the text recognized in them (for search) are kept in the app’s own folder on your Mac for the time you choose. You can turn history off or clear it at any time.
  • Text recognition and translation. Done by macOS on your Mac. The first time you translate a language, macOS may download the language itself.

What we do not do

  • The app cannot connect to the internet: it is built without network access.
  • No data is collected, sent to us or stored on our servers. There are no servers.
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No account, email or password is required.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Purchases

SnipWorks is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, Apple Account or billing details. There are no in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

Clear the history in Settings › History. Deleting the app removes its own folder; files you saved to other folders stay until you delete them.

Children

SnipWorks is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
